# No em dash

Do not use "—".

Use a normal hyphen "-" instead, or a comma, colon, or period if that reads better.

Also do not use the CJK long dash "——". Prefer "-" or "：".

Examples:

- Title: `cv-ecommerce - Vietnam briefing` (not `cv-ecommerce — Vietnam briefing`)
- Aside: `Shopify checkout early. AI later.` (not `early — AI later`)
- List label: `Shopee Uni 25329 - 6% transaction fee` (not `25329 — 6%`)
