# Sources (collected 8 Nov 2026)

Figures below are **not mixed into one model**. Marketplace GMV ≠ nationwide B2C revenue.

## Market size and platforms

- Metric four-platform GMV 2025: VND 429.7 trillion (~USD 16.35B), +34.75% YoY; 3.94B items; Shopee 56.04%; TikTok Shop 41.31%; ~601.8k active sellers (−7.43%).  
  https://en.vcci.com.vn/economic-news/online-retail-sales-in-vietnam-jump-35-to-over-16-bln-in-2025-report-114817  
  https://hanoitimes.vn/online-retail-sales-in-vietnam-top-ususd16-billion.962966.html
- MoIT / VECOM B2C ~USD 31B in 2025 (nearly 10% of retail).  
  https://vnmarketinsights.com/insights/ecommerce-vietnam/
- VECOM 2026 report (via VnEconomy): retail e-commerce USD 38.5B in 2025; 2026 projection USD 44.3B. H1 2026 four-platform GMV VND 264.8–291.6T (YouNet vs Metric). Electronics +26%, FMCG +20%, fashion +18%, beauty +12%.  
  https://en.vneconomy.vn/vietnams-e-commerce-market-records-strong-growth-in-first-half-of-2026.htm
- Q&Me May 2025–Apr 2026 window: Shopee 53% / TikTok Shop 44% of four-platform GMV.  
  https://theinvestor.vn/tiktok-shop-narrows-gap-with-shopee-as-vietnam-e-commerce-becomes-duopoly-d19240.html

## Consumers and payments

- TGM 2025: ~50% shop weekly; 92% smartphone; clothing 63%; COD still top preference (~35%); BNPL used by ~49%.  
  https://tgmresearch.com/vietnam-ecommerce-trends-2025.html  
  https://tgmresearch.com/vietnam-ecommerce-payment-shift-2025.html
- YouNet: 62.8% of digital consumers shop weekly; Gen Z/Millennial lead.  
  https://www.younetgroup.com/younet-vietnam-e-commerce-intelligence-2025-report-from-consumer-behavior-to-breakthrough-growth-on-e-commerce/
