# Sources (collected 8 Nov 2026)

Figures below are **not mixed into one model**. Marketplace GMV ≠ nationwide B2C revenue.

## Market size and platforms

- Metric four-platform GMV 2025: VND 429.7 trillion (~USD 16.35B), +34.75% YoY; 3.94B items; Shopee 56.04%; TikTok Shop 41.31%; ~601.8k active sellers (−7.43%).  
  https://en.vcci.com.vn/economic-news/online-retail-sales-in-vietnam-jump-35-to-over-16-bln-in-2025-report-114817  
  https://hanoitimes.vn/online-retail-sales-in-vietnam-top-ususd16-billion.962966.html
- MoIT / VECOM B2C ~USD 31B in 2025 (nearly 10% of retail).  
  https://vnmarketinsights.com/insights/ecommerce-vietnam/
- VECOM 2026 report (via VnEconomy): retail e-commerce USD 38.5B in 2025; 2026 projection USD 44.3B. H1 2026 four-platform GMV VND 264.8–291.6T (YouNet vs Metric). Electronics +26%, FMCG +20%, fashion +18%, beauty +12%.  
  https://en.vneconomy.vn/vietnams-e-commerce-market-records-strong-growth-in-first-half-of-2026.htm
- Q&Me May 2025–Apr 2026 window: Shopee 53% / TikTok Shop 44% of four-platform GMV.  
  https://theinvestor.vn/tiktok-shop-narrows-gap-with-shopee-as-vietnam-e-commerce-becomes-duopoly-d19240.html

## Shopify / logistics / law

- Shopify Payments not listed for Vietnam (checked via industry roundup 19 Sep 2026). Extra third-party fee on Shopify plans.  
  https://pendym.com/shopify-payments/vietnam  
  https://www.shopify.com/vn/payment-gateways?country=vn

  https://vnmarketinsights.com/insights/logistics-last-mile/

  https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/EN/Thuong-mai/Law-122-2025-QH15-Electronic-Commerce/703876/tieng-anh.aspx

## Shipping fees

- Shopee: listing price excludes buyer shipping; buyer sees fee at checkout; seller pays only weight/size discrepancy vs declared pack size. Official:  
  https://banhang.shopee.vn/edu/article/13246  
  Modes Hỏa tốc / Nhanh / Tiết kiệm / Cồng kềnh; chargeable weight max(actual, L×W×H/6000):  
  https://help.shopee.vn/portal/4/article/79534  
  International orders 7–15 days; Express International or Standard International, last-mile SPX:  
  https://help.shopee.vn/portal/4/article/79556  
  https://help.shopee.vn/portal/4/article/79651
- GHN published inner-city HN/HCMC 0–3kg: 21,000₫ (inner) / 29,000₫ (outer); other provinces inner 15,500₫. Extra +2,500₫ / 0.5kg.  
  https://ghn.vn/pages/dich-vu-giao-hang
- GHTK Express from HN & HCMC, effective 22 Sep 2026, VAT included: nội tỉnh phường 22,000₫ up to 3kg.  
  https://ghtk.vn/dich-vu-giao-hang/bang-gia-dich-vu-ghtk/
- Shopify has no Vietnam carrier rate card. “Up to 87% off shipping labels” on shopify.com/pricing is Shopify’s own label program (not GHN/GHTK). VN last-mile = merchant contract with GHN/GHTK/etc.
