Shopify vs Shopee vs TikTok Shop: overseas buy, platform fee, shipping

Same buyer job — get a China-origin product into Vietnam — three different machines. Every number below is sourced. Category rates move often; treat Seller Center as final.

They all let a VN buyer buy overseas. They are not the same product.

Shopee

Marketplace. Buyer already in the app. Overseas shops labeled Hàng quốc tế. China/HK sellers join via shopee.cn; SLS to VN; last-mile SPX.

Buyer help: international 7–15 days. Shopee Help 79556. CB seller channel: shopee.cn/seller.

TikTok Shop

Content feed with checkout. CN/HK POP can target SEA including VN via Global Selling. Korea→VN CB lane announced live Aug 2026. Local-stock shops still convert better.

Global Selling SEA (SG, MY, TH, VN, PH): seller.tiktokshopglobalselling.com. Korea lane: UpTik 2026.

Shopify

Your store, not a mall of overseas shops. Buyer only arrives if you pay for traffic. You are the importer. Shopify Payments is not in Vietnam, so every card/wallet checkout is a third-party PSP.

Payments availability: shopify.com/vn/payment-gateways. Third-party fees: shopify.com/pricing.

Platform fees (seller take-rate)

Marketplace “commission” is a stack, not one number. Shopee and TikTok both charge a category % plus a 6% transaction fee plus 3,000₫ per delivered order. Shopify charges a subscription plus 2% only when a third-party PSP is used — not on manual COD.

If the same SKU is still in China

  • Add VAT on every express parcel since 18 Feb 2025 (Decision 01/2025/QĐ-TTg).
  • Duty if customs value > ~1,000,000₫.
  • Add 7–15 days (Shopee Help) vs 1 day GHN inner.
  • Shopee still takes ~24.5% of the VND price and 6% of any buyer-paid international ship.
  • Shopify still takes 2% of a larger DDP price — cheaper take-rate, but you buy the click and you own customs.

Tax: vnmarketinsights. SLA: Shopee Help 79556.

What this means for cv-ecommerce

Shopify is cheaper as a cash register (2% or 0%) and more expensive as a customer factory (you pay CAC that Shopee/TikTok bundle into the 20%+ take-rate). Use Shopee/TikTok to see if a China SKU still wins after 24% fees + VAT + 7–15 days. Put winners on Shopify with GHN/GHTK local stock so you keep the fee gap and match 1-day inner-city ship.
