Implication

This is not “build Shopee.” Marketplace GMV is a duopoly. A Shopify store only works if AI + China sourcing produces a visible landed-price advantage after VAT, duty, freight, and slow delivery — and if checkout feels local (VND, wallets, COD).

Why “cheaper from China” is getting harder

The old loophole — express parcels under VND 1 million with no import VAT — is gone. Duty relief is still there, but a 2026 draft would almost wipe it out.

18 Feb 2025in force

Decision 01/2025/QĐ-TTg: every express parcel pays import VAT, including ≤ VND 1,000,000 (~USD 40). Press cited 4–5 million small China→VN orders/day under the old exemption.

Today (Nov 2026)current

Duty still exempt under ~VND 1M per express parcel. VAT 10% standard; many goods temporarily 8% until 31 Dec 2026. ACFTA Form E can cut China duty vs MFN (apparel/cosmetics often 15–22% MFN).

Sep 2026 draftnot issued

MoF consults on cutting the duty exemption to ~VND 100,000 (~USD 4) or tax payable ≤ VND 10,000. If adopted, almost every commercial parcel pays duty + VAT.

1 Jul 2026law live

E-commerce Law 122/2025/QH15: livestream ID, seller quality docs, platform duties. A Vietnamese-language or .vn storefront can be treated as a platform and may need registration + resident representative.

Landed-cost stack to model on every SKU

  1. China EXW (1688 / factory)
  2. Inbound freight + insurance (CIF)
  3. Import duty if parcel > VND 1M (HS code; ACFTA if C/O)
  4. VAT on (CIF + duty)
  5. Last-mile (GHN / GHTK / Viettel Post / J&T)
  6. Payment cost + COD fail/return leakage
  7. Shopify subscription + 3rd-party transaction fee

Industry rule of thumb: switch from parcel import to local 3PL stock around USD 50–100k monthly sales.

How Vietnamese buyers actually shop

  • ~50% shop online weekly (TGM); YouNet 62.8% among digital consumers.
  • 92% smartphone vs 30% desktop.
  • Categories: clothing 63%, personal care 55%, beauty 48% (TGM).
  • COD preferred by 35%; BNPL used by 49% (usage ≠ preference).
  • ~35% have joined livestream shopping; Gen Z uses micro-influencers and shoppertainment.
  • Price, speed, and easy returns sit above brand. Cross-border lose on speed/returns unless you hold VN stock.

AI web app wedge (from the chat + data)

Buyers already find cheap China goods on Shopee/TikTok. The product only wins if it does something those apps do badly:

  1. Show true VND landed price, tax included, before add-to-cart.
  2. Filter junk/fake listings and explain quality in Vietnamese.
  3. Offer local payment + realistic delivery windows.
  4. Later: hold fast-movers in a VN warehouse so speed matches Shopee.

Recommended next 30 days

  1. Pick 20 SKUs in beauty accessories, home, or fashion basics — high search, inspectable quality, no special licences.
  2. Build a landed-cost sheet (China + freight + VAT + duty if >1M + last-mile + 8–12% leak). Kill SKUs that are not clearly cheaper than Shopee after that.
  3. Stand up Shopify: VND, Vietnamese, VNPay + COD, GHN or GHTK.
  4. Do not dropship everything. Pre-stock a few winners in HCMC/HN so delivery is 1–2 days.
  5. AI v0: a VN chatbot / search that explains the product and the tax-inclusive price. Leave livestream until Law 122 processes are understood.
  6. Legal: business registration path; whether the site is a “platform”; PDPD for customer data.
